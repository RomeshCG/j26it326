# M3 — Early Warning System & Loan Lifecycle: System Usability Scale (SUS) & User Evaluation Instrument
### IMFS Research Project · J26-IT-326 (Appendix 1.1)

---

## 1. Evaluation Overview

* **Objective**: Evaluate the usability, operational clarity, and decision-support confidence of the M3 module (Early Warning System, Mobile Field Collection, and Loan Origination) among microfinance operational practitioners.
* **Target Audience**: Loan Officers (Field Staff) and Branch Managers across Sri Lankan MFIs.
* **Target Usability Benchmark**: **SUS Score $\ge 68.0$** (indicates above-average, industry-grade usability per standard Brooke 1996 psychometric norms).

---

Respondents rate each item on a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree).

### Scoring Calculation:
1. For odd questions (Q1, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q9): Score contribution = `Rating - 1`
2. For even questions (Q2, Q4, Q6, Q8, Q10): Score contribution = `5 - Rating`
3. Total SUS Score = `(Sum of contributions) * 2.5` (Yields a score from 0 to 100)

## 3. Semi-Structured Qualitative Interview Protocol

### Section A: Early Warning & Distress Interpretability
1. **Explainability**: *“When looking at an alert card, did the plain-language rationale (e.g., 'Recent on-time payment rate dropped to 33%; group arrears ratio at 26%') provide enough context to decide on an action?”*
2. **Actionability**: *“Did the recommended interventions (e.g., 48h field visit, loan restructuring, peer group meeting) match what you would realistically do in Sri Lankan field conditions?”*
3. **Lead Time**: *“How valuable is receiving an alert proactively before an account enters PAR30 versus seeing it on the overdue list only after it is already 30 days late?”*

### Section B: Field Collection & Offline Tolerance
1. *“How was your experience entering field collections and generating digital receipts?”*
2. *“Did the offline mode (saving locally when connectivity drops in rural routes) provide sufficient confidence that payment data would not be lost?”*

### Section C: Origination & Approval Workflow
1. *“How smooth was the transition from application submission to schedule generation upon Branch Manager approval?”*

## 4. Benchmark Target Matrix

| Dimension | Target Metric | Research Justification |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------|
| **System Usability Scale** | **$\ge 68.0$ / 100** | Industry standard acceptable usability threshold (Brooke, 1996) |
| **Alert Actionability Rating** | **$\ge 80\%$ positive** | Loan officers agree the plain-language rationale is operationally clear |
| **Advance Warning Value** | **$\ge 85\%$ preferred** | Officers prefer predictive alert over reactive PAR30 overdue reports |
| **Task Completion Rate** | **100% on core flows** | Field collection entry, application submit, alert acknowledgment |
