# M2 Notebooks — Guide

This folder contains the research and experiment notebooks for **Component 2 (Finance & Mission Intelligence)**.

## Notebook Overview

- **`01_mission_intelligence_and_drift_model.ipynb`**:
  1. **EDA & Baseline:** Evaluates 7,245 partner loans, PAR30 delinquency (with the >4.3 installments rule), and outreach metrics against MIX Market Sri Lanka & World Bank Findex benchmarks.
  2. **Dual-Mission KPI Engine:** Pure functional calculations for portfolio quality, interest yields, and social performance indicators.
  3. **Retrospective MDI Evaluation:** Formula implementation across the 12-month historical timeline, tracking the emergence of the July 2026 alert.
  4. **Predictive XGBoost Drift Risk Modeling:** Early warning machine learning model predicting forward drift probability and continuous MDI trajectory with feature importance analysis.
  5. **Branch Profiling & Grounded Narratives:** Risk ranking across all 17 individual branches and automated, NFR-06 compliant executive reporting.

## How to Run

1. Open in VS Code or Jupyter.
2. Select Python kernel from `ml/m2_mission_intel/.venv`.
3. Click **Run All** or execute cells sequentially from top to bottom.
